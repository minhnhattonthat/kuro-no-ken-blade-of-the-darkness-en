# Style reference - Kuro no Ken: Blade of the Darkness

Every translator matches this. Built from the pilot (02OLB01, merged) and the voice notes in `docs/synopsis.md`.
Register: dark fantasy with satire (cowardly nobles, censorship, absolute rule) and comic townsfolk. Plain modern
English, no Ye Olde except where the JP is archaic (Hiko, old men); no modern slang ("okay" is fine, "cool" is not).

## Voices
| Speaker | JP markers | English devices |
|---|---|---|
| Caius (カイエス) | おれ; terse, stoic, slightly archaic (よかろう, しかたあるまい, ～とあれば); きみ to Shinobu, きさま/お前 to foes | short declaratives, few contractions in serious moments, dry understatement: "Very well." "Then it can't be helped." "You" -> "you"; きさま -> "you bastard" only in anger, else "you" with a hard tone |
| Shinobu (シノブ) | わたし; feminine わ/の/よ/わね, ちょうだい; reserved with strangers, spunky and teasing with friends (もう、カタブツなんだから) | contractions, polite but firm; "Give me back my sword." Later, with Caius: playful scolding: "Don't overthink it! Honestly, you're such a stiff." |
| Ennis (エニス) | あたし, あんた; ～さ, ～かい, まァ, ～ンのかい, ～だね; streetwise tomboy, thief boss (姉御) | breezy, cocky, contractions and dropped words: "Relax, relax." "You got somewhere to go?" "Nabbed it." "huh?" for ～かい/～だね |
| Zephudol (ゼフュードル) | おれ/おりゃ; slurred thug speech: おめえ/てめえ, 強ェ, なりてーんだ, ～ぜ, ンだろ; crude, lecherous | "ya", "gonna", "wanna", "ain't", clipped grammar: "I just wanna be the strongest, see?" |
| Vii (ヴィイ) | 私; soft keigo, prophetic, fatalistic (運命の織糸) | formal, measured, no contractions, slightly poetic: "Nothing is ever chosen by chance." |
| Hiko (ひこ) | 我/私; archaic, grand: そなた, ～ぞ, ～ぬ, ～であろう | "I", "thou" is too much: use "you" but archaic syntax and no contractions: "I am will... I have no life." "So it shall be." |
| Lurus (ルルス) | 私; cold, then flirtatious | silky, teasing: "Ready to hear me out now?" |
| Riel (リール) | polite です/ます, earnest, young head scholar | polite, eager, a little formal: "Thank you so much for the medicine!" |
| Gordon (ゴードン) | おれ様; small-time bully | blustering: "Don't you go treatin' the great Gordon like small fry!" |
| Melfina (メルフィナ) | little ojousama ～ですの/ますの | prim, precious: "It's ever so amusing." |
| Duncan, Genhas, Yubaum, old scholar | わし, じゃ, わい, おぬし; Duncan gloats (クククッ, ざまァみろ) | old-man English: "eh?", "my boy", "I tell you"; Duncan: "Heh heh heh... Serves them right!" |

## Mechanics (see `docs/translator-brief.md`)
- 「」 -> "...". A speech that runs over a page break (`dlg` then `cont`) keeps one pair of quotes across the pages:
  no closing quote on the first page, no opening quote on the `cont` page.
- 《通行証》を受け取った -> `Received the Pass.` (no brackets around item names).
- Silence 「・・・・・・」 -> `"......"`; trailing off ・・・ -> `...`; stutter じょ、冗談 -> "K-Kidding!".
- Signs 『...』 -> '...'.
