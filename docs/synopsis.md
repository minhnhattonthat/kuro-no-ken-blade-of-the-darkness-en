# Synopsis for translators - Kuro no Ken: Blade of the Darkness

Merged from the four story readers' notes (`work/tl/story/synopsis1..4.md`). Names follow `glossary.md`. Read this before translating any batch. Late reveals are flagged "(late reveal: ...)". Do not let lines foreshadow them more than the Japanese does.

How the script is laid out: each area code (02OLB, 03YSK ...) is a map. The two-digit suffix and letter (06BLK04J, 07CSL05B) is the map's event set for one phase of the story. Most towns are revisited, so one area holds lines from early, middle and late game. Many NPC lines repeat across these sets and must be translated identically. The VIS files are the FMV/cutscene texts. They are numbered roughly in story order and are placed below where the dialogue suggests they play.

## Background (what the player pieces together)

Kreutzen sits on top of the Black Dragon, Leib-Bern, a being of destruction that sleeps deep in the Black Dragon Cave. Ages ago the black-haired clan from the East failed to kill it with holy power. Their homeland sank, so they forged the Black Sword out of demonic power as a last resort. Nobody could wield it, but the dragon fell into a long sleep. They sealed it with a magic layout of four temples (Black Dragon Cave, Maccaloc, Lake, Valley), two holy sites (Nobles' Tower, Forest Island) and two cities (Balkrutha and Zald), laid out by Eastern geomancy (Qimen Dunjia). The natives who stayed behind as the barrier's priests are the frontier folk. The dragon's Wave slowly changes them into part-monsters with powers (magic, foresight, shapeshifting).

Centuries later the Templar Knights crossed the sea from the south. They conquered the land, slaughtered and drove the natives to the Frontier, wrecked their culture and turned their sanctuary into the Nobles' Tower. Their descendants, the nobles and the settlers (移民), rule Kreutzen and censor this history. A bored, peaceful aristocracy ordered the ruins surveyed for treasure, and that began the awakening. Three seals hold the dragon: the First Seal (the Gate of Seals, a black crystal in the cave), the Second Seal (a tablet atop the Nobles' Tower, opened with Lionheart) and the Third Seal (deep below, reached by the four Lapis and opened only by the Black Sword).

## Plot by area

### 02OLB - Port Allvaine
The game opens with VIS00. A silent storm "without a drop of rain" wrecks Caius's ship. A Mystic asks why he chose Kreutzen ("there is no chance in choosing"), and he asks her Magic Mirror to show him his path. VIS02 follows: Ennis has pulled Shinobu from another wreck and wants the favour repaid. She keeps Shinobu's "strange sword" as surety and sends her, as a mage, to the nobles' mansion south of town. The port is closed "because of the storm", scholars from the capital are studying it, and a Giant Crayfish blocks the north gate to the marsh. On Caius's side, the Allvaine mayor makes him kill the Sea Troll in the marsh in exchange for a pass ("not that I insist you go"). Later visits: after the first Wave the port reopens and townsfolk flee or bet on the dragon. The nobles' exodus leaves from here. After the second Wave, Allvaine is wiped out and a lone survivor buries the dead.

### 03YSK - Nobles' Mansion
On Shinobu's side, she is hired to guard the Green Star Crystal. Ennis has tipped off the soldiers, who jump her, and the bodyguard (Zephudol) toys with her and leers. She escapes with a shikigami decoy. Shinobu was the decoy: Ennis swapped the gem for a fake. Ennis returns the sword. Shinobu names it ("the Black Sword. It must not be drawn.") and receives a Pass to Balkrutha. Zephudol is fired.
Late game (03YSK65-70): the nobles meet at Duncan's mansion to loot the country and flee by ship. Duncan says the port was closed so that only nobles could board, and that Black Ore keeps them safe. "Leave the commoners as food in Balkrutha's larder." Duke Yubaum admits "we were settlers anyway" and that he only wanted ancient treasure. Choice: take the Crystal Key (Melfina has wandered off with it) or kill him. Caius and Shinobu demand the Scale Lapis. Yubaum claims he left it at the castle. Gordon fights Caius again and sails as a guard. The old scholar's parting words: only those who stand and fight when everyone cowers may call themselves knights. An angry mob that broke the checkpoint storms the mansion. Lore books here (03YSK69B/C): the Templar Knights came "from far south". The censored "×××" means "invaders". There are four flooded, anti-magic ruins.

### 04OLD - Cave of Old Books
An old mine with scholars. A tablet in an Eastern script warns roughly "grimoire... worm... do not open". Shinobu can read it ("you get used to it"). Caius cannot.

### 05SKS - Checkpoint
This is the gate on the road to Balkrutha, run by corrupt guards. An Allvaine pass is no good here. Shinobu is jailed here early on. Caius is tricked into the same cell ("weren't you eaten by monsters?!"). After the first Wave, Balkrutha goes under martial law and refugees are turned away. Choice: bring down the hammer, pay a bribe, or give up. Later the checkpoint falls, a citizens' militia forms, and frontier folk (some can fly) join it.

### 06BLK - Balkrutha, the Capital
This mountaintop city is revisited all game.
- **00-01 (Shinobu):** A library book of folk tales tells how an Eastern prophet who warned of the sleeper below was stoned, and how knights from over the sea conquered the land. Four forbidden books are checked out. The town is all stairs and the rich live higher. There is hostility toward "them" (the frontier folk) and toward foreigners. Shinobu meets Riel at the library and warns her that the "temples" hold no wisdom. Riel tells her that a girl with Shinobu's black hair and eyes was found alive in a centuries-old coffin in the Black Dragon Cave and is kept under the castle. Riel lends the Feathered Hat as an introduction.
- **02 (Caius):** Riel's letter says the castle is closed and scholars have been arrested. Caius saves Ennis from a mercenary (VIS04, "a swordsman cuts with skill"). He takes a job from Genhas to find the curser and confronts the jokey Warlock, who escapes through a wall and promises that Caius will learn "the true face of Kreutzen". Genhas tells him of the escape tunnel into the castle. VIS05 likely plays around here: a voice, the Will of the sword's maker, offers Caius demonic power. Shinobu's shikigami cuts in ("don't touch it! help me, the Dimensional Prison in the courtyard"), and the Will tells him to seek the Black Dragon Cave.
- **03-04:** Curfew, then martial law. Zephudol reveals that he murdered their master, who meant the secret art Azure Dragon for Caius, and stole the Swordmaster's Book. Caius holds the Oral Teaching that completes it. After a first duel Zephudol defers. An informant explains the chain of command (Duncan, Glauben, Genhas, Malder, the Duke) and gives the bar password (three decoys vary one word). Overheard: Dral and Maccaloc are destroyed, and arrested citizens were sent off with explosives implanted in them.
- **05-06:** The castle is freed and martial law lifted. Black Dragon Cave survivors describe the Gate of Seals breaking and the Wave killing everyone on low ground. Riel finds that broken Black Ore becomes a silent Void that swallows its surroundings: the dragon is death and birth at once.
- **07-12 (after the second Wave):** The nobles have fled. Balkrutha is the last refuge, shared uneasily by settlers and transformed frontier folk. Side stories: Ferdinand blown up by a bomb, a cult, a pyramid scheme, a quiz. Riel's plan is "poison against poison" (Mine Key, Mercurius Cloth). Palman treats the ore and Grinwuld forges Wave-proof gear. The Warlock returns: the nobles drowned. Goetz waits at Cove Village because the Scale Lapis lies in the wreck. Riel prays to the earth spirits for their return.

### 07CSL - Balkrutha Castle
- **01 (Shinobu):** She gets in with Riel's hat and meets comic guards and Malder's swooning maids. Malder refuses to stop the dig. The old scholar explains the Templar conquest. In the basement Shinobu sees the black-haired girl in a water tank (VIS03 first half): "the same clan as me". A voice says 'I have waited for you... and the sword you carry... give me life... my descendant'. She is imprisoned.
- **02 (Caius):** The castle staff fight while asleep. Caius meets the Three Apostles, sorcerers serving "the one who governs great destruction". One summons a Raiju. They wonder whether he is the heir. He frees Shinobu from the Dimensional Prison, which opens only when someone outside wants to save the one inside. She explains the Doll ("a will and vast magic, but no soul"), Leib-Bern and the Black Sword. Caius touches the sword and it nearly takes him: "it rejected me". He offers to protect her until they get out. VIS06 follows: the Apostles let "the two" go while they hold the Doll. One of them, Lurus, recruits Zephudol in a dive inn with the promise of "the power called the Black Sword".
- **04:** Ennis loots the dazed castle. A golem replays its maker's message: the dream-eater is a Nue, and the Silver Bell stops it. After the message the golem just parrots Caius. Lurus appears and wants the "wind" swordsman as heir, so she puts Caius to sleep to eat his dreams (VIS11). Defeated, she turns out to be the dream of a woman asleep for ages, now a mummy (VIS11A: the castle wakes).
- **05:** The Duke gives a reward (sword, information or 3000 G) and promises Lionheart, which lies in the Abandoned Mine. The forbidden book "A Study of the Kreutzen Natives" lays out the seal geography. Malder's son "dies" from a mine-uchi (blunt-edge) gag ("Don't kill me off!").
- **06-08:** Lionheart is stolen. Ennis is found as an empty husk: the Doll took her body with a forbidden soul-return art of the black-haired clan, leaving the Red Crystal. Later the castle fills with the Wave. Zephudol sits on the throne beside Hiko (VIS16), wanting the Black Sword simply to be strongest, and names the duel: the hill at Dral.

### 08CKD - Balkrutha Tunnels
Caius is jailed at the checkpoint. His cell neighbour, a fence, says a black-haired woman escaped this cell by magic, and then a Murder Rock eats him. A book, "The Arms Dealer's Creed", is here. On a later visit the old scholar's lab provides the Silver Bell against dreams and illusions.

### 09HIK - Abandoned Mine
A miner who eats monsters. Ennis has tailed the heroes. Her secret is that her scent hides her ("the secret of Red Ennis"; it works poorly on women). She grabs Lionheart and leaves them to the mine's master monster. In VIS12 the Green Star Crystal set in the hilt makes Lionheart the key to the Second Seal. The Will, which looks like Shinobu to her, seizes Ennis's body to escape the Tonko Formation that binds it, and wakes with no name or memory.

### 10TNI - Valley Ruins
On the first visit (10TNI01A, after VIS03 second half), the Mystic has nursed Caius in this sanctuary. He wakes from the "mirror meditation" feeling it was a long dream and leaves ("too quiet for me"). Later (10TNI02A) the Wing Lapis and the gargoyle statue are gone, and Vii says she will wait at the Black Dragon Cave. (late reveal, 10TNI03A, played after VIS18: this is the inside of Vii's mirror, an "illusory past". The Mystic means to kill the mirror-Caius and steer the real one into becoming the Demon Lord who wakes "my Lord". He cuts through with his sword skill: "This isn't Vii!?") An old woman's granddaughter vanished ten years ago with an old mirror. A dream sent the old woman to dig under the altar, where she found "Survey Report on Ancient High Magic" (Super Ball, Valkyrie, Star Rain, Iwafune).

### 11STG / 12MRS / 13SLP - Marshland, Forest Island, Cave of the Sleeping Princess
Caius saves Riel's scholars from the Sea Troll. He already knows of "a sixteen-year-old girl named Riel" and wonders how much of the mirror vision was true. Legend: a minor lord put his daughter into eternal sleep to save her from plague, by a pact with the forest spirit. The tomb curses those who burned the holy land. The daughter's name is worn to "...s" (probably Lurus). Caius promises to move the grave into the sun.

### 14YMM - Mountain Path to the Frontier
An army scout surveys frontier villages for a punitive raid. The Roc sisters growl, repeat themselves and call each other 姉者. A frontier man: "no needless killing on the Frontier".

### 15MKR / 16MKI - Town of Maccaloc, Maccaloc Ruins
A hot-spring town played for comedy: a peeping mayor, a ditzy mage charging a golem toll, and a shaman (祈祷師) selling the Talisman of Happiness (his mantra puns on the Naumann elephant). Gordon returns with a grudge. Riel collapses from the ore (VIS07). Shinobu fetches a cure from Palman (VIS08), and Riel, barely recovered, works in the flooded ruin basement (VIS09). Her finding: the ore stains clear crystal black, so the Gate of Seals was once clear, and something behind it is pouring out. The army has marched dissenters toward it. After the first Wave: Dral is gone, monsters overrun Maccaloc, and Riel blames herself until Caius tells her to grieve later. Soldiers take the Scale Lapis "for the lord". The four Lapis open the Corridor of Chaos. A small soft scene: Shinobu tells Caius to smile, laughs at his attempt, and decides he is better as he is.

### 17DRL - Dral Village
Before the Wave, Palman tests a magic-halving poison. It just tastes bitter to Caius, but it boosts Shinobu. He trades the Black Ore for "a failure from two years ago", a cure-all. After the Wave everyone is dead except Palman, whom decades of poison have made a "living zombie". He keeps the corpses as "test subjects" (dark comedy) and later infuses the ore so it can be forged. Final visit (17DRL08A): the duel on the windy hill. Dying, Zephudol asks Caius for the Oral Teaching: "One must not cut people. Not being cut is my victory." He returns the Swordmaster's Book ("Let's go back to the dojo..."). VIS17: Shinobu says "he was like you". VIS17A: Hiko half-remembers the same refusals before the dragon slept and asks what he is.

### 18KSK - Black Ore Cave
Ore collected for the armour.

### 19IRE / 20NNP - Cove Village, Shipwreck Cove
Villagers laugh madly from mushrooms sold by a "strange woman". A Black Dragon cult is based here. Goetz, a disguised frontier man, tests the heroes ("I can't tell which way you'll fall yet"). Later, after the second Wave, he opens the way to the nobles' wreck. Ghosts include Glauben, Malder, Duncan, Genhas and Duke Yubaum ("bring me light"). Gordon dies at peace, thanking Caius. The book "Black Dragon Worship and the Legacy of Ancient Civilization" is here. Melfina survived because her father gave her the Scale Lapis. She lends it to "Sir Swordsman", and Goetz adopts her, dreaming of a land shared by natives and settlers.

### 21KTI / 22MZI - Lakebed, Lake Ruins
The Golden Needle unpetrifies gargoyles. Caius takes the Claw Lapis (a trap). A wall map links the four ruins.

### 23SOH - Sohou Village
A frontier village. Lucia's wyvern attacks, and she apologises. The Elder explains the Wave and asks whose side Caius is on: the settlers, the natives' curse, or the dragon. Directions to the tower ("the Demon Gate and the Back Demon Gate mean death"). After the second Wave (23SOH02) everyone has become a monster. Caius blames himself ("I refused Hiko, so Hiko broke the seal"), vows to use the sword even as a demon, nearly leaves Shinobu, and stays "for the promise". The last human Elder tells the Lapis lore and begs to be killed before he turns, and they grant it. Galsa, now monstrous, proclaims the natives "chosen". Lucia dies wishing she could have saved her people. Forbidden-book entries (Yahanraku, Tatenashi Armor) appear here.

### 24UMB - Seaside Village
Grinwuld sells two weapons for 5000 G. Genhas's Frontier Subjugation Force raids the village, abducts Grinwuld to forge for Balkrutha, and kills Sato and the Troll shopkeeper ("they're monsters anyway"). Caius: "Think hard about which of us is closer to a monster."

### 25TOU - Nobles' Tower
The Master Witch ambushes them. VIS13 (earlier, at the tower): Vii names the reborn Doll "Hiko" and says there are two heirs, one of wind and one of water. Hiko sets a trial: whoever climbs the tower and touches him is the heir. VIS14: Caius refuses Hiko's demonic power, because wielding the sword without it means being ruled by it, and with it means becoming a Demon Lord. Hiko opens the Second Seal with Lionheart and a far larger Wave sweeps the land. The heroes survive at the top of the tower. Galsa reads the tablet, names Zephudol the Apostles' swordsman, and turns himself into a monster. The tower sinks and the lake drains. The alchemical book Smaragdina names the dragon Leib-Bern: "life of amber", beginning and end, it dies as a dragon and revives as stone, and only what is not of this world can destroy it.

### 26KKR / 27KKI - Black Dragon Cave, Black Dragon Cave Ruins
First visit (26KKR02A, 27KKI01A): Vii warns Caius off, since the sword and its heir are what wake the dragon. Caius says the sword rejected him. Vii and Shinobu reveal that this was acceptance, because the sword dominates whoever can bear it. Caius accuses Shinobu of seeking a sacrifice, then apologises. Soldiers march abducted Balkrutha citizens to the Gate of Seals, where implanted explosives blow them up. A dying scholar asks Caius to warn his sister. Shinobu chants a talisman barrier, and the dragon's roar sends out the first Wave (VIS10: warriors drop swords, priests forget prayers, children stop crying).
Late visit: the Master Witch sells "Stone +10" and "Stick +15" and fights with Samson. VIS15 (probably here): Caius names Vii an apostle. She says the Three Apostles gave themselves to the dragon and that Hiko is to the Black Sword what the Green Star Crystal is to Lionheart. VIS18: Vii traps Caius in the mirror ("farewell, Caius"), and he wakes before the Mystic again ("where is this?"), which leads into 10TNI03A. (late reveal, 27KKI03A: when he wins, the mirror shatters and Vii vanishes, smiling. Caius guesses that Vii never existed, only a woman whose mirror-self had been killed and replaced, probably the old woman's granddaughter. "The Three Apostles are finished.") The four Lapis on the altar fuse into the Holy Stone Blade for Shinobu ("power given to the Guardian of the Sword").

### 28KDI - Ancient Stone Chambers (Zald)
The ancient underground city. The ghosts of the Four Sages (Juan Ryudo, Maccaloc, the lake demon, and the valley mage's golem) and Kreutz test Caius, and Kreutz calls him his descendant. A thief steals and returns Lionheart. Hiko waits at the great door.

### 30IZM - Sanctuary Spring
A pause by "the River Lethe" under glowing insects. VIS19 plays here: Shinobu thanks Caius and apologises for dragging him in. He says dying in battle is a swordsman's joy, swears on his name to slay the dragon, and she refuses to leave ("Don't think so hard! You stiff!").

### 29KNT - Corridor of Chaos (finale)
VIS20: at the door, Hiko has decided to kill them and wield the sword himself. VIS20A: defeated, he remembers. He is Hiko, the black-haired sorcerer who forged the Black Sword. He left his likeness with "demonic power the sword cannot rule" for an heir, and he heals them with the last of it. In the corridor the "Invited", souls lured through the Dimensional Prison, walk to their master. The sword's magic is gone, but Shinobu knows what fuels it. She draws it and hands it to Caius.
29KNT99B (ending): the sword kills even the Void. (late reveal: the sword feeds on a soul, and its source is Shinobu's life. She hid it because she did not want to see him draw it.) As her fear turns the blade black again, she begs him to finish her before she is trapped in it forever. Caius promises they will travel on and that protecting her will be his endless training. Her last words: "I... don't want to die..."

### 31END
There is no 31END text in the extracted script. The ending stops at 29KNT99B (see open questions).

## Tone

Grim, satirical dark fantasy: colonial guilt, censorship, absolute rule, suicide bombers, genocide, mercy killing, and a tragic, open ending. Keep the nobles' cowardice and the settlers' slurs (辺境のあいつら, 移民ども) ugly, not softened. Comic relief runs alongside: drunk brothers, a "50% off all year" shop, betting kids, cults and scams, the parroting golem, Palman, the Master Witch, the Warlock's SAVE-file joke, and pun items (Star Platinum, Parasite, Stone +10, Moonshadow Dried Fish). Play the comedy straight and broad. Do not wink at the grim scenes. Caius stays clipped even at his most emotional. Shinobu is bright until the end.

## Open questions

1. The opening mirror meditation: how much of Caius's early chapter is the "vision" (he knows Riel in 12MRS before meeting her)? It affects the tense and certainty of his early lines.
2. Is Vii the vanished granddaughter of 10TNI04A? The text only implies it.
3. Is Lurus the Sleeping Princess? (13SLP gives "娘・・・ス")
